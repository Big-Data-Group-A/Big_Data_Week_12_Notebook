### Project Title
**E-Commerce Price Dispersion and Automated Price Anomaly Detection Across Rwandan Online Retailers**

### Proposal (One Paragraph)
Our group investigates how retail prices for essential electronics and household goods vary across online Rwandan merchants and whether automated price aggregation can reveal systematic overpricing and regional availability gaps (**Question**). We will assemble and analyze a structured dataset of ~15,000 live product listings—recording product category, normalized specifications, listed RWF price, merchant district, and stock availability—collected via automated web scrapers and merchant APIs across Kigali commercial hubs alongside public consumer price benchmarks (**Dataset**). Answering this question matters because Rwandan consumers and small businesses face fragmented, opaque pricing across disconnected storefronts; quantifying price dispersion enables transparent comparison tools that lower household search costs, strengthen market efficiency, and promote fairer digital commerce (**Why It Matters**). We plan to implement both a supervised regression model to predict the expected fair market price of an item given its technical specifications, and a classification anomaly detector to isolate heavily overpriced listings or stockout risks (**Planned Model**).
